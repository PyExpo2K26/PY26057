Know Your Milk

Not all milk is the same. Choose the perfect profile for your family's dietary needs.

A2 Pure Cow Milk

Lighter & Highly Digestible

Naturally rich in A2 beta-casein proteins. Cow milk is thinner in consistency, easier on the stomach, and perfect for growing children and direct drinking. Lower in fat, high in essential vitamins.

Farm Buffalo Milk

Thick, Creamy & Rich

Noticeably thicker with a higher fat and calcium content. Perfect for making rich curds, traditional Indian sweets, heavy teas, and extracting thick malai (cream) for homemade ghee.
